PA App Guide

Tasks, reminders, notes and a calendar in one place, with reminders on your phone through Microsoft To Do. Everything here is what's in the app today.

Chapter 1

Start here

What PA App is

PA App keeps your tasks, notes and events in one data file in OneDrive, saved a couple of seconds after every change. You can edit on the laptop and on the phone; if both changed things, PA App puts them together. Microsoft To Do also puts your tasks on the phone with their reminders.

☀ My DayToday's tasks, what's overdue, and today's events.
☑ TasksEvery task, on a board or in a list.
▦ CalendarThe month, with tasks, events and coming repeats.
✎ NotesFree-form notes, saved as you type.
🔔 NotificationsEvery message from the last 30 days.
⚙ SettingsThe data file, backups, phone reminders, buckets and labels.

Install it on the laptop

Open PA App's web address in Chrome, then install it so it opens in its own window like any other program:

  1. Open PA App in Chrome.
  2. Click ⋮ (top right) › Cast, save, and share › Install page as app.
  3. From then on, open it from the Start menu or the taskbar.

On the phone, open the same address in Chrome, then ⋮ › Add to Home screen (or Install app) › Install. PA App then opens from its own icon, full screen.

Needs internet to open. The installed app loads from the web each time, so it won't open offline yet. Your data never leaves your computer, apart from the copy of your tasks sent to To Do.

The first time

  1. Settings › Create new data file, or Open existing data file if you have one. Put it in a synced folder such as OneDrive so the phone can see it.
  2. Settings › Choose backup folder: one dated copy a day, automatically.
  3. Open any task › Choose attachments folder for files you attach.
  4. Settings › Phone reminders › Connect Microsoft To Do, to get reminders on your phone (see chapter 5).
The sidebar says where you stand. At the bottom left, "Saved to file" means you're safe. "Browser only" means no data file is linked yet.

Chapter 2

Tasks

Quick add

Type a task the way you'd say it. PA App picks out the date, time, repeat, label, bucket and priority, and shows what it understood before you press Enter.

Pay utilities bill Friday 6pm #bills @home every month !high
→ "Pay utilities bill", due Friday 6:00 pm, monthly, label bills, bucket home, high priority
TypeMeans
tomorrow 10am, Friday, 5 Nov 4:30pm, in 2 weeksDue date and time
every Monday, every 3 days, weekdays, monthlyRepeat
#billsA label (made if it's new)
@homeA bucket that already exists
! or !high, !med, !lowPriority

If it got the words wrong (say, "Meeting notes for Friday" isn't due Friday), click Keep words as typed before pressing Enter.

Board, list, tabs and sorting

  • Board shows one column per bucket; drag a card to move it. On the phone, hold the card a moment, then drag it; near the screen's edge the board scrolls along to the next column. List is one long list that can be grouped by due date or bucket.
  • Tabs: All, Open (the default), Overdue, Done. Filter by bucket or label next to them.
  • Sort by due date, priority, name, newest, oldest, bucket or completion; the board follows it too.

The task panel

Click a task to open its panel on the right. Everything saves as you go.

StepsA checklist; a step can become its own task.
Add to My DayShows it on My Day today only.
DueA date, with or without a time. No time = any time that day.
Remind meOne or more reminders (chapter 4).
RepeatDaily, weekly, monthly… (chapter 3).
Bucket, priority, progress, start date, labelsSort and group by these.
FilesAttach bills or photos; they're copied into your attachments folder.
NoteAnything else about the task.

At the bottom: ⧉ Duplicate makes "Title (copy)", not started, with steps unticked. Delete asks first if the task has notes, steps or files.

Ticking a task off

Ticking asks when it was done: Today (just press Enter), On the due date if it was due earlier, or another date and time. A task done after its due date shows Done 3 days late.

Untick to open it again. You can change a done date later in the panel.

Chapter 3

Repeating tasks

How repeats work

A repeating task has one open copy at a time. Tick it off and its next copy appears, due on the next date, with the same steps (unticked), reminders, labels and 📌 files.

Rent (Nov) · due 1 Nov✓ tick →Rent (Dec) · due 1 Dec
  • Kinds: daily, weekdays, weekly on chosen days, monthly (by date, or "the last Friday"), yearly, every N of any of these, and again after I finish it, counted from the day you tick it.
  • Ends: never, on a date, or after a number of times.
  • A repeating task always keeps a due date: its next copies are counted from it.
  • If you miss some, the missed copies stay as overdue. To catch up instead, use "again after I finish it".

The moving part

The part in brackets moves forward with each copy, while the title stays the same: Rent (Nov 26), then Rent (Dec 26). Starting a repeat on "Pay rent Nov" turns "Nov" into the moving part for you, in the panel and in quick add.

Renaming a repeating task renames every copy, done ones too; each keeps its own moving part.

Unticking and changed copies

Unticking takes the next copy away again, unless you've changed it since it was made (notes, steps, its date…). Then PA App asks "Remove it anyway?" and says what you changed.

For "again after I finish it" repeats, changing the done date moves the next copy too. If you've changed that copy, it asks "Move it anyway?": Yes moves only its date and keeps your changes.

Files on repeats

A file attached to a repeating task stays with that copy only, like this month's bill. Click 📌 on a file to keep it on every copy instead, like an account-details sheet. A copy's History shows the earlier copies with their files.

Chapter 4

Reminders and notifications

Reminders

A task can have several reminders. Pick one from Remind me, or a custom one:

KindExample
Before the due timeAt due time · 10 min · 1 hour · 1 day before. For a task with no time, counted from 9:00 am that day.
A time of day9:00 am on the day · Evening before (9:00 pm) · 2 days before at 6:00 pm
An exact date and timeWorks even with no due date.

Each reminder goes off once. Changing the due date makes the ones counting from it go off again.

Where they ring

  • With To Do connected (chapter 5): your phone, and To Do on the laptop, ring for them. PA App just shows its own 🔔 message inside the app, so nothing rings twice.
  • Without To Do: PA App rings on the laptop while it's open.
  • Missed: reminders that went off while PA App was closed (or the laptop slept) don't all pop up when you open it. You get one message instead: "3 reminders went off while PA App was closed: …".

Notifications

Messages fade after 5 seconds. 🔔 Notifications in the sidebar keeps every one for 30 days, newest first and grouped by day, with a count of unread ones. Click a reminder there to open its task. Clear all empties the list.

Chapter 5

Phone reminders (Microsoft To Do)

Connecting

  1. Settings › Phone reminders › Connect Microsoft To Do.
  2. Sign in with your Microsoft account in the pop-up and allow access to your tasks.
  3. A "PA App" list appears in To Do with every open task. Your phone's own reminder app shows it too, if it's linked to To Do.
About once a day Microsoft asks you to sign in again. A banner says "Phone reminders are paused"; click Sign in.

On the phone there's nothing to connect: the phone's OneDrive sign-in includes To Do, and it syncs whenever it can edit, so a task added on the phone reaches To Do a few seconds later, laptop closed or not. If the phone signed in before this was added, Settings › Phone reminders › Sign in again once. Both devices keep the same list up to date and never delete or double each other's tasks.

What goes where

Done in PA AppDone in To Do
New task → appears in To DoNew task in the "PA App" list → comes into PA App
Edit, tick, untick → followsTick, untick, rename, notes, Important, due date, reminder → come back
Delete → deleted in To DoDelete → kept in PA App, not sent again
Repeat → To Do gets each copy as it comesA repeat set there → becomes PA App's repeat
  • If the same thing was changed on both sides between syncs, PA App's version wins.
  • To Do holds one reminder per task: the next one still to come. Turning it off there skips that one.
  • It syncs when PA App opens, a few seconds after a change, every 5 minutes, and on Sync now.
After restarting Chrome, click Reconnect by the data file first. Syncing waits for it, and the Phone reminders card says "Paused — reconnect the data file first".

Chapter 6

Your data

The data file

Every change is saved to the data file about two seconds later, from the laptop or the phone. Settings per device (theme, which screen is open) stay out of it.

  • Reconnect: after Chrome restarts it asks permission again; one click.
  • Both changed? If the file was saved elsewhere while this browser also has changes, PA App puts them together, task by task: the newer change to each task, note or event wins, and nothing else is lost. A message says "Brought in changes saved on …". Something deleted on one side stays deleted.
  • A different data file? Opening another data file while this browser has data of its own asks first: Keep this device's version or Use the file's version.
  • Backups: one dated copy a day in your backup folder (the phone puts its own in OneDrive › Apps › PA App › Backups). Export saves a copy any time; Import replaces everything (it asks first).

On the phone

Open PA App's address on the phone. Signed in to OneDrive, you can do everything there that you do on the laptop: add and tick tasks, edit them, notes, events, repeats, reminders. The banner says ✏ Editing, and every change saves to OneDrive a couple of seconds later.

  • It reads the latest by itself when you open it, when you come back to it, and on Refresh.
  • If the laptop saved in the meantime, the phone puts both together (the newer change to each task wins) without asking.
  • No signal or signed out: it shows the last copy, read-only. A change made just as the signal went is kept on the phone and saved when you're back.
  • Files: tap a file's name to open it. 📎 Add file or 📷 Take photo puts a new one straight into OneDrive (see Attachments below for the one-time move). ✎ renames and ✕ removes, as on the laptop; a removed file goes to OneDrive's recycle bin.
  • Phone reminders: the phone keeps To Do up to date too (chapter 5), so reminders don't wait for the laptop.
  • Backups: the phone's first save of each day also puts a dated copy in OneDrive › Apps › PA App › Backups, never over an older one. Settings › Manual backup has Export and Import on the phone too.
  • Board: hold a card a moment, then drag it to another column. A quick swipe still scrolls and a tap still opens the task.
  • The one difference: the laptop chooses its data file, the phone always uses OneDrive.
  • Without signing in, Open data file picks the file by hand, read-only.
Setting it up (once)
  1. On the phone: Sign in to OneDrive and allow access. PA App asks only for its own folder, OneDrive › Apps › PA App, and can't see your other files. Signing in makes that folder.
  2. On the laptop, in File Explorer: move your data file into OneDrive › Apps › PA App.
  3. In PA App on the laptop: it says the data file "isn't where it was". Go to Settings › Open existing data file and choose it in its new place.
  4. On the phone: tap Refresh.
About once a day Microsoft asks the phone to sign in again; it shows the last copy until you do.

Attachments

Files you attach are copied into your attachments folder and named after the task. The data file only stores their names. Removing a file from a task deletes it from the folder only when no other task uses it.

So the phone can use them (once)
  1. Close PA App on the laptop.
  2. In File Explorer, move your attachments folder into OneDrive › Apps › PA App, beside the data file. Its name must be Attachments.
  3. Open PA App on the laptop: Settings › Attachments folder › Change folder and choose it in its new place.
  4. On the phone: open a task with a file and tap the file's name.
A file added on the phone reaches the laptop's folder once OneDrive has synced it, usually within a minute.
